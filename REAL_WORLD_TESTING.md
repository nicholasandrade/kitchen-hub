## Observe

- Wake phrase succeeds on the first attempt from normal cooking positions.
- Television or music does not cause accidental commands.
- Transcription handles ingredient names and kitchen noise.
- Command confirmation appears quickly enough to maintain flow.
- Next, previous, direct-step, serving, ingredient, question, and finish actions behave correctly.
- Active recipe, step, checks, and servings remain stable through a full session.
- Questions do not lose or modify the active recipe unexpectedly.
- Ingredients, instructions, and confirmations remain readable across the kitchen.
- Routine cooking does not require touching the Surface.
- The app recovers after sleep, reboot, loss of network, and a failed request.
- Completing the recipe removes it from **Continue cooking**.

## Friction log

For each issue, record:

1. what you said or did
2. what Kitchen Hub displayed or changed
3. what you expected
4. whether retrying recovered
5. whether the recipe state remained correct

Prioritize fixes that interrupt cooking, corrupt state, require touch, or repeatedly waste time. Defer unrelated roadmap work until several full sessions are complete.
